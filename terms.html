Bookings

Appointments can be requested online through our booking form. A booking is confirmed when you receive our confirmation message. Please arrive a few minutes early so we can make the most of your appointment.

Cancellations & lateness

We ask for at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or move your appointment. Cancellations with less notice, and missed appointments, may be charged at up to the full service price. If you are running late, please call us. We will always do our best to accommodate you, although we may need to shorten the service.

Payments & pricing

Prices are shown in pounds sterling and include applicable taxes. Payment is due at the end of your appointment by card or contactless payment. Prices may change, but the price shown when you book will be honoured for that appointment.

Your information

We use the contact details you provide only to manage your appointment, send booking information and, where you have opted in, share occasional studio news. We do not sell your information. You can ask us to remove your details at any time by emailing hello@pridemuleya.co.uk.

Questions

For questions about an appointment or these terms, contact us at hello@pridemuleya.co.uk or 020 7946 0184. These terms were last updated September 2026.
