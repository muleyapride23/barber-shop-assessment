A little about us

Made for the
long haul.

We opened PMe with one simple belief: the best barber shops are built on trust, consistency and knowing your regular’s coffee order.

The beginning

PMe started with two old chairs, a borrowed set of clippers and a shared love of making people feel looked after.

Today, we are a small team of barbers working from a light-filled studio in the heart of Borrowdale. We are serious about the craft, relaxed about everything else.

Come in for the cut. Stay for the chat.

The people

Meet the team.

Three specialists.
One standard.Every barber at PMe brings their own expertise, but we share the same commitment to precision, service and making every client leave looking their best.

Marcus Dube

Master Barber

Skin Fades · Precision Cuts · Beard Sculpting

“A great cut should look sharp from every angle.”

With over 8 years behind the chair, Marcus is known for precision and consistency. His approach combines classic barbering techniques with modern styles, taking time to understand each client's look before picking up the clippers.

Tendai Moyo

Senior Barber

Fades · Textured Styles · Hair Design

“Your style should be as individual as you are.”

Tendai brings a creative edge to the PMe team. From textured crops to detailed hair designs, he enjoys creating styles that reflect each client's personality while keeping the finish clean and wearable.

Ryan Ndlovu

Grooming Specialist

Beard Grooming · Classic Cuts · Hot Towel Shaves

“Details make the difference.”

Ryan specialises in the finer details of men's grooming. His calm approach and attention to detail make him particularly popular with clients looking for a sharp beard, classic cut or traditional hot towel experience.
